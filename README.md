Welcome to the world of dragons, where myths come to life and legends soar through the skies. This book is your gateway into the breathtaking and untamed world of How to Train Your Dragon, where you'll encounter creatures of all shapes, sizes, and abilities. From the fierce and fiery Night Fury to the majestic and gentle Light Fury, each dragon has its own unique characteristics and remarkable qualities. Join us as we dive into their world, exploring their habitats, their personalities, and the deep bond they share with the brave Vikings who call them allies. Get ready to unleash your imagination—there’s a dragon waiting for you to discover.
